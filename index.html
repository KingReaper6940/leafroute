A plan you can keep.

Filters and pages change this preview only. Downloads contain the full plan. Calendar files create all-day tentative events, with no reminders; import them manually and review the result. Re-imports may duplicate or update events depending on your calendar app.

Leave a little space
around the reading.

The plan spreads whole pages as evenly as possible across your available dates, putting extra pages first. If there are fewer pages than dates, it uses the earliest dates and finishes sooner. It ignores chapter boundaries and page difficulty.

Nothing moves automatically when life changes. Update your last finished page and first reading date, then build again. This is a plan, not an activity log, a speed assessment or a promise that the workload fits your day.
